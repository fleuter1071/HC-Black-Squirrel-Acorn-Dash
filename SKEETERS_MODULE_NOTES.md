# Skeeter's Module Notes

## Purpose

This document captures user-provided research and product ideas for a future Skeeter's module or screen in `SBS: Acorn Dash`.

Use this as a durable nostalgia, design, and gameplay reference when planning a Leeds basement / Skeeter's feature.

## Core Memory

Skeeter's was remembered as a student-run pizza operation in the basement of Leeds Hall. For the game, the strongest framing is:

> basement + Leeds + student-run + late-night food + old-campus memory portal

This should feel like a specific Haverford alumni memory, especially for late-1980s and 1990s players. It should not feel like a generic pizza shop.

## What Skeeter's Was

- Student-run pizza operation in the basement of Leeds Hall.
- Later associated with the transition into The Coop / Campus Center food operations.
- Strong late-night student-life association: pizza, breadsticks, tired students, social campus energy, and old-campus nostalgia.
- Best product-language summary: student-run pizza bunker.

## Location

Place Skeeter's as a hidden basement level under Leeds.

Possible entrance triggers:

- Cracked basement door.
- Pizza-scent trail.
- Glowing acorn.
- Old flyer taped to a dorm wall.
- Leeds Key collectible.
- A glowing pizza box that pulls SBS into a retro campus memory.

Suggested prompt:

> You smell impossible pizza coming from beneath Leeds.

## Timeline Notes

Treat Skeeter's as an especially strong 1980s-1990s alumni reference, while leaving room for adjacent eras to recognize it.

User-provided source clues:

- 2015 Haverford Clerk interview says there was a student-run pizza operation called Skeeter's in the basement of Leeds.
- Student Council minutes from 1989-90 mention Skeeter's managers and budget discussions.
- 1992-93 Student Council materials suggest Skeeter's was active as a student-run business with fund/profit discussions.
- Bi-Co News material references Skeeter's Pizza and a Skeeter's employee tossing pizza dough.
- Campus Center was built in 1993, fitting the idea that Skeeter's and The Coop were later combined.

## Vibe

Skeeter's should be the opposite of polished dining.

Target feeling:

- Late-night.
- Student-run.
- Scrappy.
- Warm.
- Food-based.
- Basement-y.
- Social.
- Slightly chaotic.
- Beloved because of memory, not culinary perfection.

Scene details:

- Low ceiling.
- Fluorescent lights.
- Warm orange pizza glow.
- Pizza boxes.
- Soda machines.
- Handwritten signs.
- Handwritten menu board.
- Flour on the floor.
- Flour footprints.
- Student worker half-studying behind the counter.
- Someone burning a pie.
- Students in pajamas.
- Campus band poster.
- Box labeled: `Skeeter's Dough - DO NOT STEAL`.
- Someone asking to pay later because "Honor Code."

## Visual Design

Exterior / transition:

- Stone or dorm exterior outside Leeds.
- Narrow basement stairs.
- Pizza smell cloud around Leeds.
- Warm glow coming from below.

Interior:

- Posters layered on walls.
- Old menu board.
- Pizza boxes stacked like platforms.
- Soda spill hazards.
- Student manager counter.
- Oven glow.
- Basement stair movement path.
- Retro campus treatment if used as a time-machine memory portal.

If used as a memory portal:

- UI becomes grainier.
- Music shifts to 1990s college-radio energy.
- Students have older clothing/hair.
- Campus Center feels new, absent, or not yet dominant.
- Skeeter's is fully alive.

## Product Role

Skeeter's should be a secret unlockable basement level, not a main-map location.

Why:

- It matches the lore: beloved, half-buried, remembered, not obvious to current students.
- It rewards alumni knowledge.
- It turns food into nostalgia transport.
- It can become a memorable module without cluttering the core campus loop.

Best product framing:

> Food as nostalgia transport.

## Mini-Game Concept

### Make the Pie Before Plenary

SBS collects pizza-making ingredients before time runs out.

Collect in sequence:

1. Dough.
2. Sauce.
3. Cheese.
4. Toppings.
5. Oven ticket.

Avoid:

- Student feet.
- Spilled soda.
- Flying pizza dough.
- Hot cheese blobs.
- Pizza boxes sliding down basement stairs.
- Existential conversations in the basement.

Rewards:

- Success unlocks `Skeeter's Slice`.
- Perfect score unlocks `Extra Garlic Alumni Mode`.

## Design Recommendation

Make Skeeter's a memory portal reached through current-day Leeds.

Flow:

1. SBS finds a glowing pizza box or pizza scent trail near Leeds.
2. The game hints at a hidden basement entrance.
3. Player enters a compact retro basement level.
4. The experience shifts into a late-night 1990s campus-food memory.
5. Completing the mini-game unlocks a food powerup, collectible, badge, or share-card decoration.

This lets the feature carry nostalgia, gameplay, and future share-card value without making the main game more complex.
