# Wawa Module Notes

## Purpose

This document captures user-provided research and product ideas for a future Wawa module, side quest, or screen in `SBS: Acorn Dash`.

Use this as a durable nostalgia, design, and gameplay reference when planning an off-campus Wawa run feature.

## Core Memory

Wawa is not a Haverford-only relic like Skeeter's. It is a shared campus ritual: the off-campus convenience-store pilgrimage.

The strongest framing is:

> late night + hungry + mildly chaotic + Main Line / Philly-adjacent + emotionally more important than it should be

Wawa should not be treated as an official campus landmark. It should be treated as the gravitational field that pulls students off campus after dark.

## What Wawa Represents

Wawa represents the moment campus life spills beyond stone buildings, arboretum paths, dorms, and official traditions into the real world.

Best product-language summary:

> We are tired, it is too late, nobody wants Dining Center food, and somehow Wawa has become the answer.

For alumni, especially people who came to Haverford from outside the Philadelphia area, Wawa can also work as a regional initiation rite.

## Source Grounding Provided By User

User-provided source clues:

- Haverford itself describes a 24-hour Wawa convenience store a few blocks from campus.
- Current location data points to the Ardmore Wawa at or near Lancaster Avenue and Woodside Road.
- A Haverford Clerk Class of 2020 reflection names `countless Wawa runs` as part of shared class memory.
- Haverford first-gen / low-income student resources mention a 24-hour Wawa near campus as a practical resource.
- A Haverford sophomore program page includes Wawa hoagies as event food.
- A recognized student club description says members go to Wawa for a late-night snack after playing.
- Wawa's own history frames it as a Pennsylvania-born convenience-store chain with deep Philadelphia-area identity, with its first food market opening in 1964 and built-to-order hoagies becoming a later signature.

## Product Role

Make Wawa a bonus off-campus night level or repeatable side quest, not a normal campus zone.

Why:

- It belongs just outside the campus bubble.
- It captures a ritual rather than a landmark.
- It can add variety without crowding the main campus map.
- It creates a funny contrast with Haverford's official identity: arboretum, Honor Code, Plenary, Quaker history, beautiful campus, and then inevitably Wawa.

Best product framing:

> Off-campus supply depot.

## Level Name Options

- The Wawa Run
- Lancaster Avenue Crossing
- Shorti Quest
- A Few Blocks from Campus
- 2:07 A.M. Hoagie Pilgrimage
- The Sacred Goose

## Core Level Concept

SBS leaves campus, crosses toward Wawa, collects survival snacks, and gets back before sunrise, class, Plenary, or a student realizes they forgot their wallet.

Emotional beat:

> You are no longer collecting acorns. You are collecting survival food for sleep-deprived Fords.

## Suggested Map Flow

1. Start near campus edge.
   - SBS smells hoagies in the distance.
   - A student NPC whispers: "Wawa?"
   - Suddenly three more students appear.
2. Cross Lancaster Avenue.
   - Main danger zone.
   - Cars, bikes, delivery scooters, Blue Bus, and distracted students.
3. Reach Wawa exterior.
   - Bright lights.
   - Goose logo energy.
   - Students in pajama pants.
   - Someone carrying six drinks.
4. Use the touchscreen ordering temple.
   - Mini-game: tap the right hoagie order before the line gets angry.
5. Return to campus.
   - More dangerous because SBS is carrying snacks.
   - Bonus points if nothing spills.

## Powerups

### Built-to-Order Boost

SBS briefly customizes abilities:

- Extra speed.
- Extra jump.
- Stealth.
- Avoid all feet for 10 seconds.

This plays off Wawa's built-to-order identity.

### Hoagie Shield

SBS hides inside a hoagie wrapper and can take one hit.

### Coffee Frenzy

SBS runs faster but becomes harder to steer.

### Late-Night Snack Aura

Nearby students slow down because they are distracted by snacks.

## Hazards

- Students walking in clumps of four across the sidewalk.
- Someone holding hot coffee.
- A student staring at their phone.
- Cars on Lancaster Avenue.
- Bike lights appearing too late.
- Blue Bus jump-scare.
- A student saying, "Wait, should we get Wawa?" and changing direction suddenly.
- Hoagie line congestion.
- Someone dropping a bag of chips directly in the path.

## Visual Gags

- Wawa glows in the distance like a holy shrine.
- Students are drawn to it like moths.
- SBS sees the goose logo and briefly treats it as a rival mascot.
- The touchscreen order machine becomes a sacred altar.
- SBS carries a hoagie twice its body length.
- A "few blocks" sign keeps appearing, but the distance feels emotionally longer.
- On the return trip, SBS balances coffee, chips, and a hoagie like a tiny delivery worker.

## Broader Game Connections

Wawa can become the off-campus supply depot for future quests:

- Bring Wawa coffee to a student in Lutnick during finals.
- Bring hoagies to a club after late-night practice.
- Bring snacks back to a Customs group.
- Bring iced tea to a Haverfest picnic.
- Trade a Wawa gift card for a rare acorn.

## Escalating Repeatable Side Quest

Recommended structure:

- First run: simple snack pickup.
- Fifth run: dodge late-night student clusters.
- Tenth run: Wawa during finals week.
- Secret run: every student on campus simultaneously decides they need a hoagie.

Core joke:

> Haverford has an arboretum, an Honor Code, Plenary, Quaker history, and a beautiful campus. Also, at some point, everyone ends up going to Wawa.

## Design Recommendation

Make Wawa a repeatable side quest with escalating absurdity.

This gives the game an expandable off-campus loop without weakening the current campus-focused core. Product-manager lesson: Wawa is valuable because it is a ritual loop, not a single location reveal. That means repeatability matters more than one big set piece.
