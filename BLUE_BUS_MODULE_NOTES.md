# Blue Bus Module Notes

## Purpose

This document captures user-provided memory, visual reference, and product ideas for a future Bi-Co Blue Bus feature in `SBS: Acorn Dash`.

Use this as a durable nostalgia, design, and gameplay reference when planning a Stokes Bay, shuttle, or Bryn Mawr bonus-route feature.

## Core Memory

The Bi-Co Blue Bus is the shuttle connecting Haverford and Bryn Mawr. For generations of students, it was not just transportation. It was part of the rhythm of student life: classes on the other campus, visiting friends, going to parties, coming back tired, waiting at the stop, recognizing people on the bus, and feeling the strange in-between space of being neither fully at Haverford nor fully at Bryn Mawr.

The strongest framing is:

> Bi-Co connection + campus rhythm + social portal + trying to catch the bus on time

For the game, the Blue Bus should represent movement between two closely linked campuses: academic cross-registration, social life, and the slightly comic drama of catching the shuttle.

Ideal alumni reaction:

> Oh my God, the Blue Bus.

## Core Vibe

- Familiar.
- Slightly chaotic.
- Very campus-specific.
- A little magical.
- Part transportation, part social portal.
- A symbol of the Haverford/Bryn Mawr ecosystem.

The Blue Bus should feel like more than a vehicle. It should feel like a moving piece of Bi-Co culture.

## Product Role

The Blue Bus can serve three roles at once:

- A recognizable alumni nostalgia object.
- A gameplay obstacle.
- A world-transition mechanic between Haverford and Bryn Mawr.

This makes it especially useful because it can make the campus feel bigger, more connected, and more alive without requiring a full new game structure immediately.

Best product framing:

> A moving Bi-Co portal.

## Game Interpretation

The Blue Bus can function as both an obstacle and a portal.

SBS may need to:

- Dodge the Blue Bus as it pulls into Stokes Bay.
- Sneak aboard without being noticed.
- Ride it as a moving platform.
- Use it to unlock a Bryn Mawr bonus level.
- Collect Bi-Co items from students getting on and off.
- Avoid backpacks, shoes, coffee cups, and students rushing to catch it.

Treat the bus almost like a recurring character: reliable, iconic, slightly intimidating to a tiny squirrel, and always moving between worlds.

## Suggested Gameplay Treatments

### Campus Obstacle

At Haverford, SBS crosses near Stokes Bay as the Blue Bus pulls in. Students rush toward it, doors open, backpacks swing, and SBS has to avoid being stepped on.

### Moving Platform

SBS jumps onto the Blue Bus and rides across the map. While aboard, the player dodges rolling water bottles, sliding backpacks, dangling earbuds, and students shifting seats.

### Portal To Bryn Mawr

If SBS collects the right item, such as a Bi-Co ID, Blue Bus Token, or Mystery Course Registration Form, the Blue Bus unlocks a Bryn Mawr bonus route.

### Timed Challenge

SBS has to reach the bus stop before the Blue Bus leaves. The whole level becomes a frantic campus dash.

Possible timer text:

> Blue Bus departing soon.

## Stokes Bay

At Haverford, the Blue Bus should be associated with Stokes Bay, the campus pickup/drop-off area. This can be a recurring map location where students cluster, wait, hurry, and occasionally miss the bus by seconds.

Possible environmental details:

- Bus stop sign.
- Students checking the schedule.
- Someone running with a backpack half-zipped.
- A student saying, "Is it here yet?"
- A student saying, "I just missed it."
- SBS hiding near a curb or under a bench.

## Bryn Mawr Bonus Route

The Blue Bus can unlock a Bryn Mawr side level. SBS boards at Haverford and arrives near Bryn Mawr, where the visual world changes slightly: new architecture, different paths, new students, new hazards, and different collectibles.

This creates a fun expansion mechanic: the Haverford squirrel briefly becomes a Bi-Co squirrel.

## Tone Guidance

The Blue Bus should be affectionate and slightly funny. It should not feel like generic public transportation. It should feel like a specific campus object with emotional weight: the bus students took to classes, parties, friends, rehearsals, meetings, and meals.

The humor should come from SBS's tiny perspective. To a student, the Blue Bus is convenient transportation. To SBS, it is a massive blue beast that appears on a schedule, opens its doors, releases a wave of shoes and backpacks, and sometimes carries SBS into another world.

## Design Recommendation

Make the Blue Bus a recurring Stokes Bay event first, then promote it into a Bryn Mawr portal if players respond well.

Smallest useful implementation:

- Add a recognizable Blue Bus crossing/pull-in hazard near a Stokes Bay area.
- Add student rush behavior around it.
- Add one collectible, such as `Blue Bus Token`.
- Use the token as a future-proof unlock hook, even before a full Bryn Mawr route exists.

This keeps the current campus game simple while planting a strong expansion mechanic. Product-manager lesson: introduce the bus first as a recognizable moment, then let it become infrastructure for a larger Bi-Co world later.
